Beyond Single Points of Failure: A Financial Services Playbook for Regulatory Resilience

Financial institutions’ operational resilience is increasingly tested by aging infrastructure, vulnerable APIs, AI-powered attacks, third-party risks, and more.

Despite it all, firms must adhere to a complex web of regulations, such as the EU’s DORA and the Dodd-Frank Act, to:

  • Ensure that finserv apps and APIs are accessible to millions of concurrent users, without interruptions, at any given time
  • Ensure strict anti-fraud and data protections when faced with incidents — though their security stacks are often fragmented
  • For finserv leaders wanting to address those challenges in simpler ways, “Beyond single points of failure: A financial services playbook for simplifying regulatory resiliency” can help.
